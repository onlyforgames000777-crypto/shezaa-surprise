🤲🏻 A Dua For Syeda Shezaa 🤲🏻

اللهم بارك لها في عمرها وعلمها ورزقها
واحفظها وأسعد قلبها ووفقها لكل خير
O Allah, bless her life, her knowledge, and her sustenance... Protect her, keep her safe, fill her heart with happiness, and grant her success in every good thing 🤲🏻✨ Grant her peace when she is worried... Strength when she is tired... And endless blessings in this life and the next 🌸🩵✨ Ameen 🤲🏻
From Ahmed 🥺🫶🏻🩵💫🪄🥹
